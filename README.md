# 📊HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📌Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition data to identify key factors influencing  employee turnover.
The goal is to clean, analyze, and visualize the data to help organizations improve employee retention and workforce planning. 

## 🛠️Tools & Technology
— Python
— Power BI
— SQL (MySQL)

## 🧹Data Cleaning & Preparation
— Removed missing and inconsistent data
— Standardized employee-related fields (department, job, role, etc.)
— Verified and validated data using python and SQL

## 📊Data Analysis
— Analyzed attrition trends across department, jobroles, and demographics
— Identified patterns based on salary, experience, and job satisfaction
— Compared attrition rates across different employee segments

## 📉Dashboard (Power BI)
— Attrition rate overview
— Department-wise and job role wise attrition
— Salary and experience impact on attrition
— Employee demographics analysis

## 💡Key Insights
— Employees with lower salary levels show higher attrition rates

👉Business Action: Introduce competitive compensaton and incentive programs

— Certain departments and job roles experience higher employee turnover

👉Business Action: Focus on retention strategies in high-attrition departments

— Employee with lower job satisfaction are more likely to leave

👉Business Action: Improve workplace environment and engagement initiatives

— Mid-level experience employee show noticeable attrition trends

👉Business Action: Provide career growth opportunities and promotions
